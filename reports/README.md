# Versioned result snapshots

- [Direct arrival-feature prediction, approach-covered follow-up](direct_arrival_features_20261117/RESULTS.md): a 49k-parameter predictor replaces runtime future-simulator rollouts. New test states12–15: Fresh4 9/12, Fresh2 11/12, stale4/12, direct8/12, corrected7/12. Direct prediction improves over reuse but costs3.377ms/action including refresh/preparation, versus Fresh2 3.366ms/action. Includes249 offline pairs, evaluation-only rank32 projection,72 paired synchronous rollouts and frozen training/validation splits.
- [Initial direct-feature pilot and first-arrival failure](direct_features_20261117/RESULTS.md): training18/validation6/test12 episodes,165 offline pairs,144 synchronous rollouts on the same12 test conditions. Starting with a fresh chunk masked one immediate-arrival case; first-arrival application yielded direct5/12, corrected7/12 versus Fresh4 10/12. These failures motivated the approach-covered follow-up; they remain included.
- [PIM versus measured RTX 3090 projection kernels](pim_gpu_comparison_20261117/RESULTS.md): 48 shape/layout/dtype/vector-count variants, three randomized passes and 120 samples per variant per timing mode. Includes repeated-weight CUDA Graph, cache-flushed and eager baselines. Single-vector PIM/3090 comparison is conditional; GPU multi-vector reuse and whole-expert limitations are explicit.
- [PIM simulator execution and GR00T projection shapes](../docs/pim_simulation.md): Samsung PIMSimulator built locally; official GEMV functional check passed all 4096 outputs. Four actual checkpoint projection shapes were simulated with explicit padding and dummy data. These are single-vector kernel cycles, not full-model timing or GPU speedups. Small audit logs and the generated driver are included.
- [K16 baseline, real-time response, and action-expert PIM breakdown](k_pim_followup_20261117/RESULTS.html): 72 synchronous K comparisons, 96 measured 20Hz trials, and 48 repetitions of the same 12 conditions. Fresh4/K16 and whole25/K16 both achieved 8/12; all repeated success/stability decisions matched. Sparse correction reduced waits, but whole preparation made jointly successful trials 3.364s slower on average. Includes measured GPU stages, explicitly hypothetical PIM/overlap bounds, CSV/SVG exports and an interactive PIM calculator.
- [Predicted-future feature correction](speculative_features_20261117/RESULTS.html): 48 paired segment rollouts at 2% command mismatch, 30 exploratory rollouts at 10%, and 3 exact-future regression controls. A privileged nominal simulator predicts future observations; actual sparse MLP updates reduce whole-window post-arrival time to 35.9ms versus fresh1 41.1ms. At 2%, all arms achieved 5/6 native successes; at 10%, fresh4 4/6, fresh1/short25/whole25/unmodified replay 5/6. Correction's independent success benefit remains unproven. Includes preparation costs, dense-attention limitations and 42 saved-pair residual diagnostics.
- [RTX 3090 wrist reuse and H/K pilot](gpu_3090_wrist_20261117/RESULTS.md): 216 completed rollouts with GR00T N1.6 and SmolVLA, plus recorded-observation diagnostics.
- [Contact and containment H/K comparison](contact_hk_20261117/RESULTS.html): 180 matched contact-task rollouts and 54 plate-displacement trials. H50/K16, H50/K4, and H16/K4 achieved 53/60, 50/60, and 47/60 contact successes respectively; this does not establish a general ranking or tight-tolerance insertion performance.
- [GR00T precomputation and arrival correction](arrival_gr00t_20261117/RESULTS.html): 126 arrival-intervention rollouts and 21 terminal-segment diagnostics. Precomputation plus one fresh flow step did not outperform the fresh one-step baseline in this small probe. Oracle inputs and analytical overlap are explicitly labelled.
- [Completed first CPU pilot](local_cpu_20261117/RESULTS.md): 24 offline observations, 27 arrival interventions, 36 H/K rollouts. All 63 live rollouts completed.
- [Follow-up snapshot](hk_matrix_20261117_snapshot/RESULTS.md): prior diagnostics at 10/12 observations; H/K rollouts had not started at export. This is not the live worker.
